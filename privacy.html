2. In short

UnFog Panda records where you have travelled and clears a map at those places. All of your location data stays on your device. There is no user account, no server run by the provider, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. Map imagery, address search, routing and city boundaries are fetched from third-party services; doing so transmits the necessary coordinates and your IP address to them (see section 5).

Redrawing a route

If the device reports no position for several minutes during a journey, a gap appears in the revealed track. The app fills it with a straight line. On request it can instead ask the OSRM routing service how the road between the two ends actually runs. Only the start and end point of that gap are transmitted, never the recorded track.

This feature is off by default. You can switch it on under “Settings → Redraw route”, where you can also run it once for gaps that already exist. Nothing is transmitted for this purpose unless you ask for it.

6. Donations

The “Donate with PayPal” button under “About this app” is an ordinary link. It opens PayPal’s donation page in your device’s browser. No payment is processed inside the app, no PayPal script is loaded, and no data reaches PayPal unless you tap the button. From that point on, PayPal’s privacy statement applies. A donation is voluntary and unlocks nothing in the app.

7. Backups

“Export backup” creates a file containing your cleared points and your visit history. Where that file goes is entirely your choice through the Android share dialog. With daily auto-backup enabled, the app writes the file to the “Documents/UnFogPanda” folder on your device. The provider never receives a copy. Note that a device backup created by Android or your cloud provider may include this file.

8. Legal basis

Location processing is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given when you grant the location permission and revocable at any time in the Android settings. Fetching map imagery and routes is necessary to provide the feature you requested (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

9. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it. You can do so at any time:

  • Settings → “Reset fog” deletes all cleared points.
  • Visited places → “Delete entire history” deletes the visit history.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all stored data.

The visit history is additionally capped at the 500 most recent entries.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. As the provider neither receives nor stores any of your data, access and erasure can in practice only concern your device, where the data remains solely under your control.
